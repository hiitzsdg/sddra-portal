📐 Official Association Penalty Calculation Rule

Late charges accrue progressively for every unpaid month ($N$) that has elapsed past its monthly payment due date (e.g. members paid till July incur no penalty till 31st August; penalty applies from 1st September for unpaid August maintenance).

Cumulative Penalty = (N × (N + 1) / 2) × ₹ 100
